About this model

Resonant continuous-wave drive, perfect mode matching, and a single traveling-wave mode. This is the high-finesse (small round-trip loss) model used in the reference screenshot. All transmissions and the additional loss are power fractions, entered in ppm.

With total loss S = T₁ + T₂ + T₃ + T₄ + A and round-trip time τ = ngL/c: circulating power / incident = 4Tin/S²; net coupled power / incident = 4Tin(S − Tin)/S²; output power / incident = 4TinTout/S².

κ = S/τ is the energy decay rate; κext = Tout/τ; FSR = 1/τ; finesse = 2π/S. Escape share is Tout/S. Input and output ports are distinct because the input port also contains prompt reflection and interference.

The drive is assumed to stay on resonance. Wavelength sets the optical frequency c/λ and quality factor Q = (c/λ)/(κ/2π); the plotted quantities do not depend on wavelength when the specified mirror transmissions and group index are fixed. No material dispersion is inferred.

At 780 nm, Q = 1.54 × 10⁸.
