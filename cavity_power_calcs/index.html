About this model

Exact round-trip interference for continuous-wave input with perfect mode matching, one traveling direction, and frequency-independent mirror properties. Mirror power reflectivity is Rᵢ = 1 − Tᵢ; transmissions and additional loss A are entered in ppm. Light travels 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 1. Additional loss is lumped immediately before returning to the chosen input mirror.

Let τ = ngL/c, FSR = 1/τ, p = (1 − A)R₁R₂R₃R₄, ρ = √p, and φ = 2πΔf/FSR, with Δf = flaser − fresonance in Hz. Define D = (1 − ρ)² + 4ρ sin²(φ/2). Circulating power just after the input mirror / incident = Tin/D. Detuning is entered in MHz and held fixed during the scan; the response repeats every FSR.

If b = (1 − A)∏i ≠ inRᵢ, net coupled power / incident = Tin(1 − b)/D. Each output receives circulating power × Tout × the reflectivities of intervening mirrors. Input-port power is 1 minus net coupled power and includes prompt reflection and interference. Round-trip escape share is the fraction leaving the selected output in one pass from just after the input, divided by 1 − p.

The round-trip energy decay rate is κ = −ln(p)/τ. The selected mirror's additive logarithmic contribution is κext = −ln(Rout)/τ; its ratio to κ is only approximately the escape share at small losses. These rates diverge at zero survival. Airy power FWHM = (2 FSR/π) asin[(1 − ρ)/(2√ρ)], when a half-maximum crossing exists; finesse = FSR/FWHM. κ/2π approximates this spectral linewidth only at high finesse.

At 100% transmission on every mirror, circulating power just after input and net coupled power both equal incident power. All light exits at the next mirror, independent of detuning. There is no resonant buildup; FWHM, finesse, and Q are undefined. At zero transmission and loss, an initially empty cavity cannot be excited.

Resonance wavelength sets fresonance = c/λ and ring-down quality factor Q = (c/λ)/(κ/2π). The laser frequency is fresonance + Δf. Wavelength does not change the plotted quantities when transmissions, group index, and detuning are fixed. No material dispersion or longitudinal mode number is inferred.

At 780 nm, ring-down Q = 1.53 × 10⁸.
